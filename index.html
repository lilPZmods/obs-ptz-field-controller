Password for other computers

A password was made up when the controller was first set up. Change it here to one of your own (6 characters or more). Changing it signs out every other computer.

VDO.NINJA

Switcher feed room

The room was made up for you; VDO.Ninja needs no account. Open the director room on the computer at the switcher and share the switcher's picture and sound there. Change these only to use a room you already have.

EMAIL

Google account for invites

Not signed in.

Signing in lets the controller send invites as you through Gmail; it cannot read your mail. Google requires an OAuth client for this, made once in Google Cloud Console: enable the Gmail API, then Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Desktop app, and paste its ID and secret here. Sign in from a browser on the computer running the controller.

HOW IT CONNECTS

Pan, tilt, zoom, brightness and colour are all sent straight to the cameras as VISCA commands, like a hardware joystick controller. Red and blue gain need the camera in manual white balance; brightness needs the Bright exposure mode.
